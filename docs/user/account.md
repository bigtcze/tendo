# Your account and sign-in

Open **Your account** from the home screen to see which login you are signed in with.

## Sign in with your identity provider

Some households set up a shared sign-in service, such as Pocket ID. When the administrator has turned it on, the sign-in screen shows a **Sign in with …** button below the password form. Your password always keeps working, even if that service is down.

Before the button works for you, connect it to your Tendo account once:

1. Sign in with your Tendo login and password.
2. Open **Your account** from the home screen.
3. Enter your current Tendo password and select **Connect …**.
4. Approve the request at the identity provider. You come back to **Your account**, which now says your account is connected.

After that, **Sign in with …** takes you straight to your household.

## What connecting does not do

- It does not create a Tendo account. A person who is new to the household needs an invite link from an owner first; see [Household members and invitations](household-members.md).
- It does not add anyone to a household.
- It does not match accounts by email address.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| This sign-in isn't connected to a Tendo account yet. | Sign in with your password, then connect it from **Your account**. |
| That sign-in is already connected to another Tendo account. | Each provider account can be connected to only one Tendo login. Use the Tendo login it is already connected to. |
| Sign-in was cancelled. | Try again, or use your password. |
| That sign-in attempt expired or was already used. | Start again from the sign-in or account screen. |
| You signed in or out in another tab while this was in progress. | Start again in one tab. |
| … can't be reached right now. | The identity provider is unavailable. Use your password. |
