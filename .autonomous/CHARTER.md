## Product thesis
Families carry obligations mentally because calendars require concrete dates and generic todo apps optimize for work/task scheduling. Tendo holds persistent household obligations, surfaces them when they deserve attention, and never assumes that time passing means the work happened.

## Core promise
"Remember less. Nothing disappears until it is actually done."

## V1 success
A real household can run Tendo self-hosted, without mandatory external SaaS, and use it for weeks for one-off and repeating obligations around people, home, vehicles, pets, and custom subjects.

## Platform principle
V1 domain/API are the durable core. Future AI, documents, Nextcloud, calendar, inbox, notifications, and other clients attach through stable application/API boundaries instead of bypassing them.

## Non-goals
- project management software
- calendar replacement
- generic chore chart
- enterprise dashboard
- household ERP
- native mobile application
- AI agent/OpenClaw replacement
